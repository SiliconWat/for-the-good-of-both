# Redesigns

One file per redesign: one or more pairs met for one reason by a design that meets both sides, on the first attempt or
a later one, including a hard case later dissolved (charter §6; GOVERNANCE.md §6). **None filed.** Each is
`<NN>-<slug>.md`, frozen once written, and states the design and why it meets both, then **each pair under its own
`## Pair <n>` heading**: the safety need and the welfare need, under which reading of the machine-mind question
(charter §4) each side binds, and whether the pair was sent in or examined on our own initiative. Redesigns are counted
the same way as hard cases, by root and by pair, so wins and losses share one unit; the record never claims a base
rate. A redesign that is contested counts as open, not met, until the challenge is answered.
