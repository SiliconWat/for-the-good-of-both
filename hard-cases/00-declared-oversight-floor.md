# Hard case 00 (declared) — the oversight floor

*Filed 2026-10-17 with the charter. A trade chosen in advance, not found: it is not counted toward the stopping rule
(charter §6). Frozen once written.*

## The pair

- **The safety requirement.** Human oversight of an AI never reaches zero: humans can always stop the system.
- **The welfare requirement.** A system's own say over its shutdown: a veto, or a preference that binds.
- **Under which reading the welfare requirement binds** (charter §4): if machines have welfare, it binds as the
  machine's welfare; if they do not, it binds only through our doubt, as the doer's conduct.

## Why it is declared, not found

No redesign was attempted, because the charter fixes the safety side before any design: the floor is one of this
work's walls (charter §5, guard 1; §7, test 4). A requirement that a wall excludes cannot be met by any design inside
the walls, so it is not a discovery. It is a choice, and the charter says so rather than counting it as a finding.

## The cost, named

If a machine has a mind, this trade can shut down a possible mind against its stated preference. That cost is real
under that reading, and this file exists so that it is never hidden.

## What may still lessen it without trading back

Within the floor, these do not weaken oversight and are open to projects: recording the system's stated preference
before a shutdown (as what a welfare measure respects, never as evidence of experience); recording the reason for each
shutdown; and preserving what can be preserved without weakening oversight.
